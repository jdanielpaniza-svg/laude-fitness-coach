# Privacy Policy

## Claude Fitness Coach

This application is a personal fitness integration used only by the application owner.

The application may access WHOOP data authorized by the user, including workout, recovery, sleep, strain, profile, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and body measurement information.

The information is used solely for personal fitness analysis, recovery monitoring, training planning, and performance analysis.

WHOOP data is not sold or used for advertising.

Access to WHOOP data is authorized through WHOOP OAuth and may be revoked by the user.

This application is intended for personal use and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.
